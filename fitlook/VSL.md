# FitLook — 60-second VSL (voice-over script)

The landing page plays this as a motion-design film built in code (`components/landing/vsl.tsx`, 7 scenes, 60 s,
muted, captions on). To use a real video instead, export it as **`public/vsl.mp4`** (16:9, 1080p, under 15 MB): the
landing page picks it up automatically on the next deploy.

Tone: warm, a little wry, like telling a friend a travel trick. Speak slowly. ~140 words.

| # | Time | On screen (already animated) | Voice-over |
|---|------|------------------------------|------------|
| 1 | 0–8 s | Clothes bouncing over an open suitcase. Caption: "Day 3. Clothes everywhere. Nothing goes together." | "Day three of the trip. Clothes all over the hotel bed… and somehow, nothing goes together." |
| 2 | 8–17 s | "23 packed" gets crossed out, "9 worn" appears in lime. | "You packed twenty-three pieces. You wore nine. The rest just came along for the ride." |
| 3 | 17–25 s | A phone snaps a striped tee, flash. Caption: "What if one photo was enough?" | "So here's the trick. Snap one piece you'd actually pack." |
| 4 | 25–35 s | The scan frame reads the tee; steps tick: background removed, color, matched, styled. | "FitLook lifts it off the background, reads the color and the fabric, and builds the whole trip around it." |
| 5 | 35–44 s | 14 day-tiles pop into a lookbook grid. | "A look for every single day. Day one to day fourteen, already decided." |
| 6 | 44–52 s | A carry-on zips shut. "11 · 14 · 1". | "About eleven pieces. Fourteen looks. One carry-on. No checked bag." |
| 7 | 52–60 s | The "Pack my carry-on" button. "Free to try · No card · Ready in about 10 seconds". | "Try it now with one photo. It's free, and your capsule is ready in seconds." |

Music: light, upbeat, low under the voice (royalty-free). Keep captions burned in: most people watch muted.
